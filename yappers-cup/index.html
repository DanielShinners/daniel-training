Kicking goals for men's health

The Yappers Cup is a community footy match built around a simple idea: bring mates together, have a kick, and create space for conversations that matter.

Founded in 2025, the inaugural Yappers Cup brought together more than 30 players, along with friends, family and supporters, for an afternoon of footy, connection and plenty of friendly rivalry — all in support of men's mental health and Movember.

Mini game · Warm up before the bounce

Play: goal-kicking practice

Pick your team, then drag back from the ball to line up your kick and let go. Six points for a goal, one for a behind. You've got 60 seconds from your first kick, then put your name on the leaderboard. Kick a couple from straight in front and the angles get tougher. Kick three and the wind comes in.

FAQs

Where to park, what to bring and how we play.

Where is it?

Gosch's Paddock, off Olympic Boulevard next to AAMI Park in the Melbourne & Olympic Parks precinct, on the Richmond side of the river. Open in Google Maps.

Bounce is 1pm on Sunday 29 November. Please arrive by 12:30pm.

Where do I park?

Park around the Tan and walk across the river to the paddock, or find a spot on the streets around Richmond.

Public transport works too: Tram 70 from Flinders Street or Richmond stations stops at AAMI Park on Olympic Boulevard, or catch the train to Richmond Station and walk.

What should I bring?
  • Footy boots or runners
  • A mouthguard

We'll likely head to the pub for a beer after the game.

What are the rules?
  • Mudguts v Pissants, 18 a side with rolling interchange.
  • Two 20-minute halves.
  • 6 points for a goal, 1 for a behind.
  • No sling tackles.
  • No dickheads.
Who can play?

Anyone 18 or over who's fit enough for a run. All skill levels welcome, from ex-local footy legends to people who haven't kicked a footy since school.

How much does it cost?

Every position is a $25 donation to Movember. Pick your team and position on this site, then donate on the Yappers Cup Movember page with the same email. We reserve your spot for 24 hours while the donation comes through.

Can people come and watch?

Yes, and please do. Supporters are free. Bring a chair or a rug and cheer from the boundary, and leave your details in the supporters form so we can keep you posted.

What if it rains?

We play through the rain.
